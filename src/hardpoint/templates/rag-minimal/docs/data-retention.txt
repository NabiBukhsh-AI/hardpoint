Data retention

Northwind keeps request logs for 30 days and audit logs for one year. Audit
logs record who created, rotated or revoked an API key, who changed a billing
setting, and who invited or removed a member.

Deleting a workspace schedules all of its data for deletion after a 14 day
grace period, during which the owner can restore it. After the grace period
the data is removed from primary storage immediately and from backups within
35 days.

Customers on the enterprise plan can export audit logs to their own storage
bucket daily.
