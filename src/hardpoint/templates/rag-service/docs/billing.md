# Billing

Northwind bills monthly in arrears for the usage recorded in each workspace.

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month and are payable
within 30 days. Download past invoices from **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
Each invoice lists usage per project, so costs can be allocated to teams.

## Payment methods

We accept credit cards and, on annual plans, bank transfer. A failed card
payment is retried after three days and again after seven. After the second
failure the workspace owner is emailed and the workspace moves to read-only
after a further 14 days.

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 30 days of an invoice for unused prepaid credit.
Usage that has already been consumed is not refundable. Contact support with
the invoice number to request a refund.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the month.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.
